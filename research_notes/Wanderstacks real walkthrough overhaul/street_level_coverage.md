# Street-level and interior 360 coverage, 52 libraries (Google Street View, Mapillary, Panoramax; state: November 2026)

Method: no Google or Mapillary key was exported (`env | cut -d= -f1 | rg -i 'mapillary|google|maps'` returned nothing). I drove the `agent-browser` CLI against the public web apps. Google: opened each library's coordinate in Maps Street View mode and read the panorama the app snapped to (URL and screenshot). Mapillary: opened the web app at z19 at each coordinate in two isolated browser sessions (one with the 360-only filter `panos=true`, one without) and counted Mapillary-green coverage pixels inside a 100 m radius circle; pixel counts are a crude presence measure, not an image count. Panoramax: public search API (no key), 100 m box (about +/-0.0009 deg lat) around the coordinate. KartaView: API did not respond from this machine (timeouts) and I did not query its web app; every KartaView cell is "not checked". Not repeated: Commons panoramas and Sketchfab (see captures_libraries_1_26.md and captures_libraries_27_end.md in this folder).

## Which libraries can get a real outside view and a real inside walk from Google, Mapillary and Panoramax

### Takeaway
Every Google hit this method found is a user-contributed photo sphere (contributor name shown, e.g. "Pietro", "Point 360", "samayoukodomo"), none is a connected interior walk, and official Google car coverage could not be tested (see Gaps). Mapillary gives usable outside imagery for roughly half the list but 360 panoramas for only a handful (Longroom area, Klementinum, Stockholm, Peabody, Morgan, Sainte-Geneviève). Panoramax has real 360 sets only at BnF and Peabody.

### Inferences
- Best outside-view candidates by evidence: Prunksaal, Klementinum, Stockholm, Palafoxiana, Sainte-Geneviève, Peabody, Morgan, Buda, Ottawa, Admont, Joanina (Mapillary coverage and/or a Google sphere within about 10 m).
- A Google user sphere within about 10 m of a building-centre coordinate (Admont, Strahov, Celsus, Prunksaal, St Gall, Melk, Vatican, Ottawa, Stockholm, Klementinum, Escorial, Joanina, Pergamon, Palafoxiana, BPL and others) is probably at or inside the building. These are isolated spheres reached by Maps' snap, not shown to be a connected tour, and I did not check whether any is an official Google Arts & Culture tour.
- Only BnF and Peabody have real 360 sequences on Panoramax (157 and 48 GoPro Max or 360 items by my EXIF filter); Peabody and Klementinum also show 360 coverage on Mapillary, so those are the strongest candidates for a CC-licensed outside walk.

### Gaps
- Official Google Street View car/trekker coverage: not determined. The Maps web app snapped only to user spheres in all 52 tests, including places that certainly have road coverage (State Library Victoria, central Melbourne, returned black at the building centre and at 70 m N/S/E/W). Check each candidate in the Maps embed or with the Street View metadata endpoint once a key is available.
- Interior walk connectivity: not checked for any library.
- KartaView: not checked (API unreachable from here).
- Mapillary 360 pixel counts for Longroom match the non-filtered count exactly (7847 each), so that area is entirely 360 imagery by this method; elsewhere the pano count is lower than the total. Counts include any green-like map pixels, so treat small values (under about 200 px) as uncertain.
- Haeinsa: Maps returned a sphere but its coordinates were not in the URL, so distance is unknown. Library of Congress: Maps jumped to Chandigarh, so no sphere within snap range was found; Mapillary shows 14 px only.

## Libraries with no usable street-level coverage at all

### Takeaway
Nothing on any of the three services was found for gabinete, wiblingen, nineveh, timbuktu, vikramashila and malatestiana (haeinsa unresolved). Alexandria, BnF-area and State Library Victoria are not empty: they have Mapillary or Panoramax data or a nearby sphere.

### Cited Findings
- Nothing on any of the three services (Google none at centre and 70 m N/S/E/W, Mapillary 0, Panoramax 0): gabinete, wiblingen, nineveh, timbuktu, vikramashila, malatestiana, haeinsa (Google location unparsed). Source: the table above.
- Ruin and remote sites with only a Google user sphere nearby and nothing else: celsus (3 m), pergamon (8 m), herculaneum (27 m), dunhuang (48 m), nalanda (70 m E).

### Inferences
- Nineveh, Timbuktu and Vikramashila are consistent with restricted or remote sites; Gabinete, Wiblingen and Malatestiana are urban, so their empties more likely reflect the Google snap limit than true absence (see Gaps in the first section).

### Gaps
- I did not verify that "none" means no official Street View; only that no user sphere or Mapillary or Panoramax image was found.
